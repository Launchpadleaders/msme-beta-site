LLBizAssess™ - MSME 360° assessment

Where Does Your Enterprise Stand?

LLBizAssess™ is a comprehensive MSME assessment tool. It scores your maturity across eight dimensions using a proprietary framework grounded in ZED, the ITC SME Competitiveness Grid, IFC SME Governance Methodology, and Baldrige; checks your compliance against 50+ government regulations; and matches you to 200+ government schemes. Calibrated for how Indian MSMEs actually operate — benchmarked scores, India-specific rules, matched schemes, real roadmap — your path to Transformation-Ready.

  • Know where you stand — a maturity score benchmarked against your sector
  • See what's holding you back — dimension-by-dimension findings, each with a specific fix
  • Get ahead of what could go wrong — sector-specific risks, and how to mitigate each
  • Unlock the funding you qualify for — government schemes matched against your exact profile
  • Find exactly where you are exposed — a live regulatory watchlist with real penalties and a fix-it-in-order roadmap
  • Walk away with a plan — a phased 0–12 month roadmap, ready to execute
  • Take it anywhere — a downloadable report for your bank, board, or growth consultant
