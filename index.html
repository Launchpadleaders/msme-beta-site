1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Launchpad Leaders ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website and platform (Diagnose, Develop, and Deliver), whether you're an MSME owner, a forum or institute representative, an onboarded consultant, or simply browsing as a visitor. By using our platform, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the platform:

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, business name, phone number, email address, role (MSME / Forum / Consultant)
Business & financial dataTurnover, bank statements, GST returns, existing loans, collateral, receivables, and other data you enter into our diagnostic tools
Diagnostic resultsAssessment scores, flagged gaps, and recommendations generated by tools such as the 360° Business Assessment, Cybersecurity Readiness, or DPDP Compliance Readiness
Programme & engagement dataCohort enrolment, cluster participation, consultant engagement history and status
Usage dataPages visited, tools used, and general interaction patterns on the platform

We collect this directly from you (when you register, complete an assessment, or enrol in a programme) — we do not purchase or scrape business data from third parties.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate your diagnostic assessments and reports
  • To match you with relevant cohorts, clusters, or consultants
  • To operate consultant engagements, including sharing relevant diagnostic context with a consultant you've been matched or referred to
  • To communicate with you about your account, assessments, or programmes
  • To improve the accuracy and relevance of our tools
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and compliance obligations

We do not use your business or financial data to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

4. When we share information

We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With your consent — for example, when your diagnostic results are shared with a consultant you've chosen to engage
  • With co-delivery partners — Forums, institutes, banks, or state bodies you're enrolled with may see programme-level data for cohorts they administer, not your individual financial details
  • With service providers — vendors who help us operate the platform (hosting, payments, communications), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process

We never sell your personal or business data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. How we protect your information

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limiting who can view your data, and role-based permissions so that, for example, a consultant only sees the diagnostic context relevant to an engagement they're matched to — not your full account. No system is completely immune to risk, and we continually work to strengthen our safeguards in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

6. How long we keep it

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the services you've requested. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain it for legal, tax, or regulatory reasons.

7. Your rights

Consistent with the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing at any time
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may limit some platform functionality.

9. Children's privacy

Our platform is intended for business owners, professionals, and institutional representatives. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the revised version here with an updated effective date, and for material changes, we'll make reasonable efforts to notify registered users directly.

About Us

Democratising growth for 79 million businesses — 1 in 5 on Earth.

India's MSMEs have always grown the hard way — without the tools, benchmarks, and networks bigger companies take for granted. We exist to close that gap, at the scale of an entire ecosystem.

Our Vision

We envision an India where the growth tools and networks once reserved for the largest players are democratised for every MSME — closing the gap to sector standard, and growing beyond, at the scale of an ecosystem.

Our Mission
